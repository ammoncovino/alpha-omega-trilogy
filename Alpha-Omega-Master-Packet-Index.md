## How It All Fits Together

The Alpha-Omega trilogy (Books One, Two, Three) is the core book — now compiled as a 158-page print-ready PDF. Everything below is the **operational system** that surrounds the book. These are the smaller files that have meaning when all put together: governance rules, lens tools, AI routing protocols, refusal boundaries, outreach packages, and supporting frameworks.

**The system has 5 layers:**

1. **The Book** — Alpha-Omega Trilogy (print-ready PDF, done)
2. **The Governance Layer** — Rules for how the lens operates, stays locked, and gets distributed
3. **The Lens Tools** — Portable analysis frameworks anyone can use (Meaning Lens, Business Lens, Structural Lens)
4. **The AI Layer** — LLM routing, refusal grammar, and canvas prompts that let AI carry the lens
5. **The Supporting Materials** — Narratives, frameworks, outreach, and thread archives
